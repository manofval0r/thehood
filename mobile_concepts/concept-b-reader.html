The Reader

What it is. The department’s site as a printed course reader that happens to be interactive — Newsreader serif, warm paper, deep green ink. Countdowns become timetable entries with dot leaders; comments become footnotes in the margin. Why it fits. Universities run on paper: syllabi, handouts, exam scripts. Nobody else in Nigerian higher-ed tech looks like this. How it moves. Pages cross-fade like leaves turning; the annotation sheet lifts like a slip of paper.
