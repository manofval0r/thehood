The Console

What it is. The current brand, taken seriously as a handheld instrument: pure-black ground, phosphor-green data, Geist Mono for anything measured. The phone becomes an ops console for one class cohort. Why it fits. Cybersecurity students live in terminals; the flip clock is already a device they recognise. How it moves. Screens slide like app sheets, sheets snap up from the bottom, nothing fades politely.
