The Index

What it is. The department bulletin board, rebuilt as a machine — off-white newsprint ground, black ink, acid-green highlight, Archivo Black shouting. Every screen is a filing card from the registry. Why it fits. Nigerian campus noticeboards are loud, physical, taped-up artefacts; this is that, digitised without losing the volume. How it moves. Hard cuts, slam-in cards, zero fades — motion with impact.
